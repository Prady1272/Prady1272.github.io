## About Me

Hi! I'm Pradyumn, a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [UMass Amherst](https://www.umass.edu), advised by [Prof. Evangelos Kalogerakis](https://kalo-ai.github.io).

My research lies at the intersection of computer vision, computer graphics, and machine learning. I am interested in 3D/4D vision and generative modeling, with the broader goal of enabling machines to understand, create, and interact with 3D worlds—not just their geometry and appearance, but also their dynamics and physical behavior. My work so far has explored various aspects of 3D shape understanding, generation, and animation.

My motivation for research comes from both practical challenges and pure intellectual curiosity. While scaling is undoubtedly powerful, I find it particularly exciting when a clever idea, an elegant formulation, or a fresh perspective can reveal possibilities that were previously overlooked.

I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science from UMass Amherst. Outside research, I enjoy playing squash and tennis.
